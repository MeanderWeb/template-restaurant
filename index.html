Our Story

Fresh Gulf Flavors Since [YEAR_FOUNDED]

The Pelican Grille opened in 2016 with one mission: bring the freshest Gulf seafood to a comfortable, welcoming spot in Cape Coral. We source locally whenever possible, support Cape Coral fishermen, and cook everything from scratch.

From first dates to family dinners, we've been honored to be part of Cape Coral's dining scene for nearly a decade. We're not a chain — we're your neighbors, and we treat every guest like family.
